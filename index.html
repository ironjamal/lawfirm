About the Firm

A Legacy of Principled Legal Practice

Nour & Partners was founded on a clear conviction: that exceptional legal counsel requires both rigorous analytical thinking and genuine commitment to each client's objectives. Based in New Cairo, we serve a diverse clientele ranging from emerging entrepreneurs to established corporations and high-net-worth individuals.

Our approach is deliberately measured. We take the time to understand the full context of every matter — commercial, personal, or regulatory — before crafting a strategy. We believe that the best legal outcomes are built on thorough preparation, transparent communication, and unwavering ethical standards.

We practice in Arabic and English, reflecting the bilingual nature of Egypt's legal and business environment.
